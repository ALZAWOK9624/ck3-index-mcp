# Mechanic design and balance

Read for requested design or balance work. These are review prompts, not grounds to refuse the user's design or expand a repair into a redesign.

Describe what the player should experience and what state changes. If meaningful choice is intended, identify the situations in which different choices are useful. A single-option notification, incremental reward, linear chapter, or optional flavor system can be intentional.

## State, incentives, and costs

Compare bounded allocation, accumulation, and depletion models against the requested fantasy. A fixed-sum influence pool can prevent maximizing every faction, while independent progress tracks can serve growth. Neither is universally superior.

Work through ordinary, best, and worst cases. Count stacking over buildings, characters, provinces, and repeated applications; distinguish local effects from global receivers. Check caps, rounding, thresholds, resets, and upkeep. Choose the magnitude and asymmetry of rewards/penalties deliberately instead of imposing an arbitrary percentage cutoff or equal punishment.

Use current indexed examples as scale references. Account for duration, prerequisites, opportunity cost, eligible population, and the surrounding Mod's balance. Frequency and a vanilla percentile are context, not engine rules.

## Legibility and ownership

Show enough state to explain available actions and their consequences. Keep a formula in one scripted source where feasible, and make displayed values track it. Hidden information may be part of the design; distinguish intentional uncertainty from a misleading tooltip.

Prefer an existing surface when it fits; see [systems and surfaces](systems-and-surfaces.md). A custom panel is appropriate when it serves the requested interaction or comparison. Evaluate readability at actual scale rather than enforcing a fixed number of values or screens.

Review initialization, save upgrades, succession, cancellation, and cleanup. Identify which scopes own the state and which entry points can change it. Estimate AI evaluation and traversal costs; measure material performance claims.

Present concrete findings and tradeoffs. Do not turn aesthetic preferences such as round numbers, symmetric penalties, or zero-sum design into correctness blockers.
